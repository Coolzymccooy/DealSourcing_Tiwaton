Automated Deal Sourcing & Analysis

From RSS feeds to ROI calculations, Tiwaton automates the boring work so you can focus on making decisions, raising finance and closing deals.

What We Do

Tiwaton is a private platform for sourcing, screening and evaluating UK property deals. Built specifically for investors and sourcers, it automates the slowest parts of the property investment process.

1. Automated Deal Sourcing

The platform collects property listings automatically using multiple RSS feeds and data sources. Every new listing is parsed for:

  • Price
  • Postcode and location
  • Bedrooms & property type
  • Potential renovation keywords

This removes the need to repeatedly browse portals such as Rightmove or Zoopla.

2. Deal Analysis Engine

Each property is analysed using UK-specific investment logic. The system automatically calculates:

  • Gross & net rental yield
  • Cashflow models including voids & maintenance
  • Flip profit and ROI
  • SDLT and buying costs
  • Refurbishment estimates
  • Finance costs and loan-to-value assumptions

Deals are tagged as strong Rentals or strong Flips based on your thresholds.

3. Personalised Criteria & Filters

Users can customise their investment profile by setting:

  • Minimum yield or ROI
  • Maximum purchase price
  • Preferred regions or postcodes
  • Flip profit expectations
  • Refurbishment budgets
  • Finance and tax assumptions

This allows Tiwaton to surface only the deals that genuinely fit your strategy.

4. Alerts & Notifications

When a new listing matches your criteria, you receive:

  • Email alerts
  • Instant Telegram notifications

This gives investors and sourcers a competitive edge in fast-moving markets.

5. Exports & Reporting

Filtered deals can be exported to CSV or Excel for:

  • Investor packs and deal summaries
  • Client presentations
  • Internal tracking and CRM systems
  • Compliance and audit trails
