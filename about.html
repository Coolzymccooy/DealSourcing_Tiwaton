About Tiwaton

Tiwaton is a UK-focused platform designed to streamline property deal sourcing, analysis and decision-making for investors, deal sourcers and estate agents. The platform automates the process of discovering new opportunities, evaluates deals using proven financial models, and delivers instant insights that help users take action faster and with greater confidence.

In a market where investors spend hours scanning portals and spreadsheets for viable deals, Tiwaton bridges the gap by combining:

  • Automated property data collection from RSS feeds
  • Smart deal scoring based on UK rental & flip metrics
  • User-driven filters & investment criteria
  • Instant alerts for high-performing deals
  • Clean data export and reporting tools

Our mission is simple: to give investors the speed and accuracy required to operate successfully in today’s fast-moving UK property market.

The Strategy Ladder

Property investment sits on a progression path—from low-capital entry strategies to advanced developments. This “ladder” helps investors understand where they currently fit and which deals match their resources:

  • Deal Sourcing – low capital, high time
  • Buy-to-Let (BTL) – foundational long-term strategy
  • Buy-to-Sell (Flip) – fast capital recycling
  • HMOs – cashflow optimisation
  • Conversions – equity and uplift potential
  • New Build & Land – advanced development strategies

Tiwaton focuses on the strongest part of the pyramid for scalable deal sourcing: Buy-to-Let, Flips, HMOs and Small Conversions—delivering solid returns with manageable risk.
